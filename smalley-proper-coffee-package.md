# Smalley Coffee × Proper Coffee
### A Proposal to Formalize What's Already Working
**Presented by Josh Premuda | Smalley Coffee | Jasper, Indiana**
**July 2026**

---

## The Short Version

Proper is already roasting Smalley's coffee. The relationship works. The question isn't whether there's a fit — it is whether we're ready to point it at growth.

This proposal is about formalizing the relationship: absorbing Smalley's brand, wholesale accounts, and digital infrastructure into Proper, and creating a defined role for Josh to run the two channels Proper doesn't currently have someone focused on — regional wholesale development and a subscription product.

The groundwork is laid. This is about building on it.

---

## Background: Four Years in the Making

In April 2022, Josh approached Parlor/Proper with a formal proposal to build a digital subscription service. At the time, he outlined four possible structures — contracted build, staff hire, Smalley as contract roaster, or full acquisition — and made the case that the same thing he'd be making today: Proper has the roasting depth and the brand; Josh has the digital infrastructure and the growth instincts to build a channel Proper doesn't have.

The conversation didn't move then. But the relationship did. Proper became Smalley's roasting partner. The Shopify storefront got built. The wholesale accounts got developed. The subscription lineup got named and tested.

Four years later, the infrastructure exists. The ask is simpler than it's ever been.

## The Role

Josh joins Proper as the operator responsible for two things: growing the southern Indiana wholesale market and managing the subscription channel end-to-end.

This is not a passive handoff. It's an active growth role with a clear lane.

### Wholesale Development

Prospecting, outreach, and onboarding of new café, restaurant, and office accounts across Jasper, Evansville, and surrounding markets. Existing Smalley accounts transition with continuity.

Josh manages the relationships. Proper handles the production. The division is clean.

**What this looks like in practice:**
- Ongoing outreach to café and restaurant prospects in the southern Indiana corridor
- In-person account management and relationship development
- Onboarding new accounts into Proper's fulfillment workflow
- Quarterly account reviews and retention

### Subscription Channel Management

Day-to-day operation of a Proper subscription product built on the Smalley lineup:

- Product curation and lineup management
- Shopify storefront operations and digital merchandising
- Customer communication and retention
- Fulfillment coordination with Proper's production team
- Subscriber growth through digital marketing and referral programs

Josh built this channel. He runs it. No onboarding curve, no ramp time.

## Why Subscriptions, Why Now

A subscription channel is the highest-leverage next step for any specialty roaster. Subscriptions compound — each new subscriber adds recurring revenue without recurring acquisition cost. Churn is manageable when the product has personality and the customer service is attentive.

The Smalley lineup maps directly to this model. The Shopify infrastructure is in place. The only missing piece is a dedicated operator to run it full-time.

That's the role.

## What Proper Gains

- Immediate entry into a warm regional wholesale market (Jasper, Evansville, southern Indiana)
- A launch-ready subscription product with built-in brand personality
- Shopify infrastructure already operational — no build cost
- A growth operator with four years of direct experience running exactly this business
- No onboarding curve — Josh already knows the coffee, the production workflow, and the customers

## What Josh Is Asking For

A defined role at Proper with two clear lanes: wholesale development and subscription management. Compensation tied to the value he brings — open to a structure that reflects growth outcomes.

The Smalley brand, accounts, and digital infrastructure transition to Proper. Josh's job is to make sure that transition drives growth, not just continuity.

The 2022 conversation was about whether there was an opportunity. There was — and it's still there. The difference is that now the infrastructure is built, the accounts exist, and the person asking has four years of proof that he can execute.

The groundwork is laid. The relationship is there. This is about pointing it at growth.
