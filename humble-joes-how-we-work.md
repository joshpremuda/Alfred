# How We Work Together
### Smalley Coffee × Humble Joe's Coffee Co.

---

The goal is simple: you focus on roasting great coffee, I focus on selling it. Neither of us gets pulled into the other's lane.

Here's what that looks like in practice.

---

## The Division of Work

**Humble Joe's handles:**
- Roasting and packaging all Smalley orders
- Shipping directly to customers and wholesale accounts
- Inventory communication (letting me know when we're running low)

**Smalley handles:**
- All customer relationships and communication
- Wholesale account management (in-person check-ins, new account development)
- Marketing, brand, and e-commerce
- Ordering green beans and packaging (shipped to you)
- Billing customers and paying Humble Joe's per production run

Neither of us is doing the other's job. That's the whole point.

---

## How Orders Flow

**Online orders (DTC):**
Shopify receives the order → order routes to you → you roast, pack, and ship → customer gets their coffee. I handle any customer questions or issues on the back end.

**Wholesale orders:**
I place the order in Shopify with the wholesale account's shipping address → you fulfill it the same as any other order. Simple queue, no special process.

**Inventory:**
We agree on a reorder point for each blend. When stock gets low, you flag me. I order greens and packaging, ship to you, and we stay ahead of it.

---

## How the Money Works

- I own the brand and keep all customer revenue
- I pay you a per-pound roasting and fulfillment fee, invoiced on an agreed schedule
- My job is to grow the top line. Your fee grows as volume grows.
- We're both motivated by the same thing: more orders

---

## What I Need From This Partnership

I'll be honest about where I am. I've built Smalley to a point where it has real accounts, a real product, and a real customer base. What I don't have is the bandwidth to also manage production.

I need a partner I can trust to handle that side without me having to manage it closely. That means:
- Consistent quality on every roast
- Orders going out on time
- A heads-up when something's off before it becomes a problem

If those three things are true, I can spend my time doing what actually grows this business — landing new accounts, building the brand, and growing the subscription channel. That's where I need to be focused.

---

## What You Get From This

- A steady and growing production contract
- No sales, no marketing, no customer service
- A brand with real wholesale accounts and an e-commerce operation already running
- A partner who's motivated to grow volume — because your revenue grows with it

---

## How We Start

I'd suggest starting small and building trust before we formalize anything. A trial run of a few months, a handful of blends, and a manageable order volume. If the quality is there and the process works, we grow from there.

No big commitments up front. Just good coffee and a process that works.
