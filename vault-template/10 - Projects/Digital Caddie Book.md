# Digital Caddie Book

A digital version of the traditional caddie yardage book. Course management, notes, and data for serious golfers.

## Status
Active — early concept

## The Idea
Golfers carry yardage books with handwritten notes about each hole — distances, pin positions, wind tendencies, shot shape notes. This is a digital version that a caddie or serious amateur can build out over time.
