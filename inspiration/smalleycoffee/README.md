## Project: The Global Cafe

**Domain:** smalleycoffee.com  
**Concept:** "The Global Cafe" — a place to buy coffee, find something to read,
and get lost for a few minutes each morning.

**Aesthetic:** Newspaper. Editorial. Black ink on cream paper.
  - Masthead-style header
  - Column grid layouts
  - Serif headlines, tight leading
  - Line-art illustration (see brand character sheet in this folder)
  - No stock photography — illustration only

---

## Brand Character

The Smalley mascot is a single recurring character drawn in retro line-art style:
bold black outlines, white fill, simple expressions. He appears across the site
in contextual roles (roaster, barista, golfer, hiker, DJ, desk worker).

---

## Content Pillars

### 1. Shop — Coffee
  - Whole bean, ground, subscriptions
  - Roast profiles, origin stories
  - Merchandise

### 2. Crema
  - Coffee discovery content (origin spotlights, brew guides, tasting notes)
  - Named after the golden layer on a perfect espresso pull
  - Editorial voice: knowledgeable but not precious

### 3. The Paper Filter
  - The morning read — curated links, short essays, dispatches
  - Newsletter format; also lives on-site as an archive
  - Tagline candidate: "Your morning paper, filtered through coffee."
