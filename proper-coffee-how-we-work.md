# How We Work Together
### Smalley Coffee × Proper Coffee

---

Proper already roasts Smalley's coffee. This is about going further — bringing Smalley's brand, accounts, and digital infrastructure into Proper, and putting someone in place to actually grow those channels.

Here's what that looks like.

---

## What Transfers to Proper

**Wholesale accounts**
Active relationships across Jasper, Evansville, and surrounding southern Indiana. Restaurants, offices, and local retailers who already trust the brand and buy consistently. Same coffee, same face, seamless transition.

**The Smalley product lineup**
Nine named blends built for subscription and retail. The identity and positioning travel with them.

**Digital infrastructure**
A functioning Shopify storefront with an existing subscription setup, customer data, and order history. Proper inherits a working channel, not a build project.

---

## What I Do at Proper

Two lanes. That's it.

**Wholesale development**
I prospect, pitch, and onboard new café, restaurant, and office accounts across the southern Indiana corridor. I manage the existing Smalley accounts through the transition and keep growing from there. Proper handles production and fulfillment. I own the relationship.

**Subscription channel**
I run the Proper subscription product day-to-day — the lineup, the Shopify storefront, customer communication, fulfillment coordination, and growth. I built this. I know how to run it. No ramp time.

Proper roasts and ships. I sell and manage. Clean division, no overlap.

---

## How the Money Works

**Compensating the asset transfer**
The accounts, brand, and infrastructure have real value. That transfer should be compensated separately from my role — either as an upfront payment, an earnout tied to revenue from those assets over an agreed period, or a combination of both. Open to discussing what makes sense for Proper.

**Compensating the role**
- Base salary covering the time and focus the role requires
- Commission on new wholesale accounts opened
- Percentage of subscription revenue generated
- Bonus tied to agreed growth targets

The structure should reflect that I'm not just doing a job — I'm building a channel Proper doesn't currently have, with assets I'm bringing to the table.

---

## What I Need From This

A clearly defined role with two lanes and the autonomy to run them. I don't need to be involved in production decisions, and I don't want Proper involved in my sales and customer relationships. That's where the partnership works — each side doing what they're actually good at.

What that looks like practically:
- I manage all customer and account-facing communication
- Proper fulfills orders reliably and flags anything that affects timing or quality
- We agree on a fulfillment and inventory process up front so it runs without daily back-and-forth
- I report on account growth and subscription performance on an agreed cadence

The goal is a channel that runs well without requiring constant coordination. I've built it to work that way.

---

## How We Start

I'd suggest a defined first term — 12 months — with clear targets for wholesale account growth and subscription revenue. At the end of that period, we evaluate and decide what comes next.

That gives Proper a low-risk way to see what the channel can do. It gives me a clear runway to prove it.
