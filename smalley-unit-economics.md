# Smalley Coffee — Unit Economics at Justin's $4/lb
### What you actually keep per bag

*Inputs: green coffee $5.10/lb, Justin's fee $4/lb (roast + package + ship-labor), you pay actual shipping. Roasting loses ~15% weight, so 1 lb roasted needs ~1.18 lbs green (green cost ~$6.00 per finished lb).*

---

## The 5lb Wholesale Bag (87% of your volume)

| Line | Cost |
|---|---|
| Green (5.88 lbs green × $5.10) | $30.00 |
| Justin's fee ($4 × 5 lbs) | $20.00 |
| **COGS (before shipping & fees)** | **$50.00** |
| **You sell it for** | **$56–60** |
| **Gross margin** | **$6–10/bag (11–17%)** |

**Then you still pay shipping and platform fees out of that $6–10.** After those, the 5lb wholesale bag is close to break-even.

---

## The 12oz Retail / DTC Bag *(prices assumed — confirm)*

| Line | Cost |
|---|---|
| Green (0.88 lbs green × $5.10) | $4.50 |
| Justin's fee ($4 × 0.75 lb) | $3.00 |
| **COGS** | **$7.50** |
| **Sell for (~$16–18?)** | $16–18 |
| **Gross margin** | **~$8.50–10.50/bag (~55%)** |

---

## The Finding That Changes Everything

**Your highest-volume product is your lowest-margin product.**

- **5lb wholesale** = 87% of volume, but only ~11–17% margin → nearly break-even after Justin's fee
- **12oz DTC/retail** = small volume, but ~55% margin → this is where the money actually is

At $4/lb across the board, piling on more wholesale volume barely moves your profit. **Growing DTC and subscription is worth several times more per pound.** The fee structure flips your growth priority.

---

## What To Do About It

**1. Push for a *tiered* fee — this is your strongest ask.**
A 5lb bulk bag is far less packaging labor than filling many small retail bags — one big bag vs. dozens of little ones. So it should cost *less* per lb, not the same. Ask Justin for:
- **~$2.50–3.00/lb on 5lb bulk** (easy to pack)
- **$4/lb on retail/DTC** (more labor per lb)

Dropping the bulk fee to $3/lb adds **$5/bag** straight to your margin — on ~1,100+ wholesale bags/year that's **~$5,500+/year** back in your pocket. This is the single highest-leverage number in the deal.

**2. Look hard at your wholesale price.**
$56–60 for 5lb is ~$11–12/lb. Specialty wholesale often runs $12–16/lb. Even a $4–6/bag increase flows almost entirely to margin. Some of your accounts may have room.

**3. Confirm the fee basis with Justin — this is a ~$3.50/bag swing.**
Is $4/lb on **finished/packaged** weight ($20/5lb bag) or **green** weight ($23.50/bag)? Nail this down; it materially changes the math.

---

## Why This Matters for the Partnership

Thin margins mean **there's very little profit to split in a JV** at current scale and mix. That reinforces two things we already landed on:
- Your income should come from **royalty + sales commission (revenue-based)**, not a share of a thin profit pool
- **Growth — specifically DTC/subscription — is the only real path to money**, for both you and Justin

The $4/lb offer is workable *if* you tier it, protect your wholesale pricing, and lean growth toward DTC. Flat $4/lb on a wholesale-heavy mix leaves almost nothing.
